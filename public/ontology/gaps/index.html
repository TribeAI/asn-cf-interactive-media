Catalogue Coverage & Gaps — what MS Learn teaches, and what it doesn't

Supply-side view of the full MS Learn module corpus: every module's extracted skills, merged into topic clusters with module-coverage counts. The competitor lens tests the catalogue against the vocabularies surfaced in the ASN taxonomy competitive research (DataCamp, Udemy, OpenAI Academy, Anthropic Academy, and the Microsoft-frontier topic slice no external taxonomy names). For the gaps-first foldable drill-down, see the gap explorer.
Reading gaps: a short red bar means the field teaches this but MS Learn barely does — a candidate catalogue gap. Hover any bar to audit the embedding matches behind the number before trusting it. Coverage = count of distinct modules whose extracted skills match the term (cosine ≥ 0.60, MiniLM).
Competitor data is a sample, not a census: the external vocabularies here are subsets captured during the ASN competitive research (DataCamp, Udemy, OpenAI / Anthropic frames, frontier slice) — not any competitor's complete topic catalogue. Read the competitor lens as directional, not exhaustive.
