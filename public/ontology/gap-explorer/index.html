Topic Gaps Explorer

A view of the full ASN corpus compared to other tools. Companion views: coverage map · ontology graph.

Gaps vs the field — Pluralsight head-to-head

A direct, absolute comparison: each topic shows how many Pluralsight courses teach it versus how many ASN modules do (the high-fidelity source — Pluralsight's full catalog, extracted the same way as ours). Sorted by gap size — where ASN trails Pluralsight most, first; topics where ASN already matches or leads the field are covered and sink to the bottom. The curated field vocabularies (DataCamp, Udemy, OpenAI, Anthropic, frontier) follow below. Open a topic to see every matched ASN cluster and its source modules with evidence.

Catalogue topics — all extracted clusters

Every topic cluster in the extraction, grouped by domain — nothing sampled, nothing hidden. Open a topic for its full provenance: all source modules with ASN role tags and evidence quotes. Topics taught by ≤ 4 modules carry a thin badge; dense pockets of related thin topics mark areas the catalogue has only begun to cover.

ASN roles — coverage by official role taxonomy

The official ASN role tags (from the catalog metadata, joined per module — not Tribe-derived). Sorted largest gap first — least-covered roles on top, so the rolled-up role families (which aggregate the most content) settle to the bottom. A family rolls up the deduped union of its members' modules and topics purely to report content overlap; membership overlaps (a role can sit in several families — data-engineer is both Engineering and Data). The family groupings are Tribe-defined (the catalog roles themselves are flat). Open a family to see its member roles, a role to see its topics, and a topic to audit the source modules with evidence.

Solution areas — coverage by Microsoft's 7 published areas

Microsoft's published solution-area themes (one per learning object, joined per module — not Tribe-derived), sorted by corpus module count. Open an area to see every topic taught inside it, sorted by coverage; open a topic to audit the area's own modules with evidence. Thin tails inside an area are area-specific gap candidates.

Sources — external taxonomies with capture provenance

Each external source's own vocabulary, rendered in its native shape, with first-class provenance: who captured it, how, when, and whether it's exact or directional. Every term is matched against the MS topic clusters (same embedding methodology as the gaps tab); open a term for its per-area coverage, its matched topics, and — for Pluralsight-derived topics — the source courses it came from.
