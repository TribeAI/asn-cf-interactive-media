Gap Explorer — where the MS Learn catalogue is thin

A foldable, gaps-first view of the full-corpus extraction. Section 1 tests the catalogue against the field's vocabularies (from the ASN taxonomy competitive research); section 2 surfaces thin areas inside the catalogue itself. Every number drills down to the matched topics, their merged names, and real example modules with evidence quotes — so each claimed gap can be audited in two clicks. Companion views: coverage map · ontology graph.

1 · Gaps vs the field

Each group is one external vocabulary; rows are sorted gaps-first by MS Learn module coverage. Open a term to see exactly which extracted topics were counted (with match similarity), then open a topic to see its merged names and example modules with their evidence quotes.

2 · Thin areas inside the catalogue

Topics taught by 4 or fewer modules, grouped by domain (domains with the most thin topics first). Some are legitimately niche; dense pockets of related thin topics mark areas the catalogue has only begun to cover.
